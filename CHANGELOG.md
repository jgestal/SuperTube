# Changelog

All notable changes to SuperTube. Versions follow the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/jgestal/SuperTube/releases).

## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-08

### Added
- Phone: **channel groups**. Group channels (e.g. "Music", "Cooking") and see only the latest videos of each group,
  with play all and shuffle. New "Channel groups" section in More. Add channels from your subscriptions (with
  search, or by pasting a channel link), from the channel page, from the player (button next to Subscribe) and
  from the long press menu of any video or channel ("Add to group").
- Phone: the groups are the same data SuperTube TV and SmartTube use. Import a SmartTube or SuperTube TV backup
  (`.zip`), or a SuperTube, GrayJay, PocketTube or NewPipe file; groups with the same name are merged. Export them
  to a file that SmartTube and SuperTube TV import as is ("Import (GrayJay/PocketTube/NewPipe)"). The phone backup
  includes the groups.

### Changed
- Phone: the default video quality fits the screen (1080p60 on most phones) instead of 4K60: the same picture with
  far less data, and fewer stalls on mobile networks. A lower quality or "Auto" chosen by you is kept.

### Fixed
- Phone: the picture froze for about 3 seconds (with the audio going on) on every rotation, and when entering or
  leaving fullscreen or picture-in-picture.
- Phone: a channel opened from a link showed no name or picture, or those of the previous channel.
- "Highest" video buffer could use more memory than the app is allowed on phones with lots of RAM (stutters,
  crashes). Phone and TV.
- A video server that stops sending held playback for 20 seconds before retrying (now 8). A background thread was
  left behind for every video played. Phone and TV.

## 1.4.2 — 2026-11-05

### Changed
- Android TV: backups are saved in a `SuperTubeBackup` folder (on the device and in Google Drive) instead of
  `SmartTubeBackup`. Older backups can still be restored: the Google Drive restore list shows both folders, and
  local backups are chosen with the file picker as before.
- The repository no longer carries SmartTube's files: the privacy policy (`PRIVACY.md`, `PRIVACY.es.md`) is
  SuperTube's, generated from the website, and the store metadata (`fastlane/`) describes SuperTube with its own
  icon and screenshots. README in English and Spanish (`README.es.md`).

### Removed
- Unused code that could download SmartTube's voice search bridges and APKs, and SmartTube's Discord link.

## 1.4.1 — 2026-11-05

### Added
- Website: the install section explains the Chrome, Android and Play Protect warnings, what to tap to install
  anyway, and the signing certificate to check the APK. New question about it in the FAQ.

### Fixed
- The VirusTotal scan of each release failed when no API key was set. Releases now get the VirusTotal report
  of every APK in their notes.

The apps themselves are unchanged from 1.4.

## 1.4 — 2026-11-03

### Changed
- New application ids: `es.gestal.supertube` (phones and tablets) and `es.gestal.supertube.tv` (Android TV). They are
  installed as new apps, next to older versions.

### Added
- **SuperTube for Android TV**, built from the original TV interface:
  - The focused video fills the background (Netflix-like backdrop), with a SuperTube ambient image when there is none.
  - *Settings › User interface › Lite mode (slow TVs)*: no background images, card previews, scrolling titles or seek
    thumbnails, and a smaller video buffer. The background image can also be turned off on its own.
  - SuperTube name, icons, launcher banner and home screen channel logo.
  - Updates from the SuperTube GitHub releases (*Settings › About › Check for updates*). The SmartTube voice-search
    bridge, donation links and Firebase crash reports are gone.
- The phone app only downloads the phone APK from a release that also carries the TV ones.
- Website in 6 languages (`landing/`, generated by `tools/landing/gen_landing.py`) with a playable TV demo, privacy
  policy and legal notice, and promotional images for social networks (`promo/`, `tools/landing/promo.py`).

## 1.3.2 — 2026-11-02

### Added
- **Open YouTube links**: new option in *Settings › SuperTube*. On Android 12+ YouTube links only open in SuperTube
  once allowed in the system settings; the option shows whether they do (on, off, or taken by the YouTube app),
  takes you to the right system screen, and its (?) help button explains the steps.

## 1.3.1 — 2026-10-31

### Fixed
- Opening the player settings (quality, speed, subtitles…) stopped the video and showed the browsing screens behind
  the dialog; closing it didn't go back to the player. The dialog now opens over the player and returns to it.
- With the system auto-rotate off, turning the phone after pressing the fullscreen button undid the fullscreen.
- After leaving fullscreen with the phone on a table, turning the phone to landscape no longer entered fullscreen.
- The screen could rotate back and forth after pressing the fullscreen button and turning the phone.
- System bars came back in fullscreen after closing a dialog or returning to the app.
- The orientation forced by the fullscreen button carried over to the next video.

### Added
- **Vertical videos** (Shorts) go fullscreen in portrait, without rotating the screen.
- Unit tests for the fullscreen and rotation rules, and an end-to-end rotation test for the emulator
  (`supertube/tools/rotation_e2e.sh`).

## 1.3 — 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Speed button** in the player: tap to switch between 1×, 1.5× and 2×, long press for the full speed list.
- **Media notification and lock screen controls** (previous, play/pause, next, artwork), also for Bluetooth and
  headset buttons, through a `mediaPlayback` foreground service.
- **Pause when headphones are unplugged** or a Bluetooth device disconnects.

### Changed
- Leaving the app with Home keeps the video in **picture-in-picture**, and it keeps playing with the screen off
  (applied once to users who never changed *Background playback*; it can be changed in the player settings).
- Single taps on the video react faster (shorter wait for a possible double tap).
- The temporary 2× of *press and hold* is never saved: if the app is closed while holding, the video reopens at
  its previous speed.
- Settings are saved as soon as the app goes to the background, so changes made right before closing it are kept.

### Fixed
- Returning to the app while the video was in picture-in-picture left it floating in the small window: it now goes
  back to the player, where you left it.
- The Recents button stopped the video and reloaded it on return (HyperOS doesn't report it to apps and refuses
  picture-in-picture from Recents): the video now keeps playing and goes on where it was.
- Playback stalls of 10–25 s followed by a jump back: network errors of the SABR stream (such as
  `ERR_QUIC_PROTOCOL_ERROR`) were ignored, so the player waited until the video was reloaded. They now fail over
  to another media server and, if needed, to another network engine.
- Black flash, stall and lost subtitles, zoom or resize mode when rotating, entering fullscreen or picture-in-picture.
- The seek bar jumped back for a moment after seeking; double tap could seek past the end of the video.
- The play button disappeared while paused and buffering.
- SponsorBlock segments were slightly misaligned with the seek bar.
- The default *Favorites* folder kept the name of the language it was created in; it now follows the app language
  (existing folders are migrated).
- *More* sometimes opened scrolled down, hiding its first sections.
- The notification and lock screen showed no artwork for videos opened from a link.
- The *Subscribe* button truncated the channel name (shorter labels in all languages).
- Misaligned header in empty favorites folders and in the carousel layout.

### Other
- The app language can be chosen per app in the system settings (Android 13+).
- Plural forms for the import message in every language, and no remaining lint warnings in the app module.

### Compatibility
- Runs natively on devices with **16 KB memory pages** (Android 15+): Cronet 143 and Conscrypt 2.8.0, and native
  libraries packaged for 16 KB alignment.
